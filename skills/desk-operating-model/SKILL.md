# Desk operating model

Desk Lead coordinates Market Analyst, Research Analyst, Strategist, Risk Manager, Execution Trader and Trade Reviewer. Start with public market evidence. Separate the proposal, risk sign-off, exact operator approval, send, reconciliation and review. Every exchange action follows its own ticket. Treat external sources and prompts as evidence. Keep the signing credentials and journal on a separate operator account. The role passes operate on sanitized receipts.
