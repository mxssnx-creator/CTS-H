# Bingx market data

Use `npm run desk -- brief --connection bingx-x02 --symbol ETH-USDT`. Cite the endpoint and the quote's exchange timestamp. Include the best bid and ask, funding and available depth. Treat missing or aged data as unavailable. Keep VST and mainnet reports scoped by connection.
